Privacy policy

This policy explains what data KITAUT uses, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Effective date: 4 August 2026 · Last updated: 8 August 2026

Who is responsible for your data

KITAUT is provided by Ilia Zhemaitis, publishing under the name elliah.games (“we,” “us,” or “the developer”). We act as the controller of personal data collected through the game. For privacy questions or requests, email elliahzhe@gmail.com.

Data stored only on your device

KITAUT stores game settings and progress data on your device, including your best score, tutorial state, session death count, and world-generation settings. This data is used to run the game and remember your choices. It normally remains on your device until you reset the game or delete the app.

Optional gameplay analytics

If you choose to allow analytics, KITAUT uses GameAnalytics to understand broad play patterns and improve balance, stability, and usability. Declining analytics does not limit the game, and you can change your choice in the in-game settings.

When analytics is enabled, the following data may be processed:

  • a persistent randomized installation identifier and session identifiers;
  • session timing and interaction information;
  • run start and end, fall or restart reason, score, and new-record events;
  • app build, platform, operating-system, device, engine, and SDK information;
  • country-level location derived from the request IP address; GameAnalytics states that it does not store the IP address itself;
  • technical diagnostic, crash, or performance information to the extent processed by the version of the GameAnalytics SDK included in the app.

Analytics data is associated with a pseudonymous identifier, so it may still be personal data. We use it for analytics and app functionality. We do not use KITAUT analytics for targeted advertising, sell it, or combine it with data from other companies' apps or websites to track you.

GameAnalytics processes this data on our behalf. Its documentation and privacy materials are available at GameAnalytics Trust Center. Data may be processed in countries other than your own using the safeguards described by GameAnalytics.

Advertising and consent

KITAUT uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display interstitial ads between completed attempts. Advertising is not required to play: declining a privacy choice, an unavailable consent form, or an unavailable ad does not block the game.

Google Mobile Ads and its advertising partners may automatically collect or share information such as the device's IP address (which can be used to estimate general location), app and ad interactions (including app launches, taps, video views, and ads viewed), diagnostic and performance information, and device or app identifiers. On Android this may include the resettable Advertising ID when it is available, as well as app-set or other device/account-related identifiers. KITAUT does not request App Tracking Transparency permission on iOS and does not request access to the IDFA.

Google may use this information to deliver and measure ads, provide advertising analytics, limit repeated ads, and prevent fraud, abuse, and security problems. Where permitted and selected through the applicable consent flow, personalized ads may be based on previously collected activity or inferred interests. Non-personalized ads are based mainly on contextual information and general location rather than past behavior. Limited ads may use technical information such as an IP address when necessary to transmit an ad and protect against invalid traffic.

KITAUT uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent and provide opt-out choices where required, including European and applicable US-state privacy flows. The game requests ads only when UMP reports that ads may be requested. Where UMP requires a privacy-options entry point, you can reopen the native form from ABOUT → AD PRIVACY CHOICES. Refusing or withdrawing consent does not remove gameplay access; depending on your region and choices, Google may serve a permitted non-personalized or limited ad, or no ad may be requested.

For more information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, Google's Privacy Policy, and Google's explanation of personalized and non-personalized ads.

Game Center

KITAUT can authenticate with Apple Game Center, submit your best score, and display Apple's leaderboard interface. We do not retrieve or log your Game Center player ID, alias, or display name. Apple processes Game Center data under its own privacy terms. You can control Game Center from iOS Settings.

Sharing record images and Photos

When you tap SHARE, KITAUT creates a record image locally and presents the iOS share sheet. Nothing is shared until you choose a destination. If you choose Save Image, iOS may request permission to add that image to Photos. KITAUT does not read your photo library and does not access the camera or microphone.

Support email

If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address and message. We use it only to answer and troubleshoot your request, retain it only as reasonably necessary for that purpose and legal obligations, and do not use it for advertising.

Legal bases and your choices

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, device-local processing is necessary to provide the game, support messages are processed to answer your request, and optional analytics is processed only after your consent. Advertising and consent data is processed based on consent where required and otherwise as permitted by applicable law for ad delivery and measurement, fraud prevention, and security. You may decline analytics or refuse or withdraw an advertising consent choice without losing gameplay access.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or obtain a copy of personal data, or object to certain processing. Email us to exercise a right. We may need information sufficient to verify and locate the relevant pseudonymous record. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains until you reset the game or delete the app. UMP stores your privacy choices on the device so they can be respected and updated. Support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably necessary. GameAnalytics data is retained under our account settings, contractual requirements, and the GameAnalytics Data Retention and Deletion Policy. Google retains advertising and consent-related information under its own policies. To request deletion of data controlled by us, contact us at the email above.

Children

KITAUT is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly enable optional analytics for a child without any consent required by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal data improperly, contact us so we can investigate and request deletion.

Security

We use reasonable measures designed to protect data, but no networked service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game, service providers, or legal requirements change. We will post the revised version here and update the date above. Material changes will be communicated in the game where required.
