Quick help

Controls

Tap in rhythm to build momentum. Swipe right to release. Swipe down to slide lower on the rope.

Game Center

Sign in to Game Center in iOS Settings. Your new local best is submitted automatically while signed in.

Sound or haptics

Open the in-game pause menu and check the audio and haptic settings. Also make sure Silent Mode is not suppressing sound.

Share a record

Tap SHARE on the result screen. KITAUT creates an image locally and opens the iOS share sheet.
